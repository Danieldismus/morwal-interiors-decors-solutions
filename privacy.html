Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Morwal Interior Decors Solutions (“Morwal Interiors,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about our services.

Information you share with us

This website does not currently have an account system or contact form. If you choose to contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp, you may share details such as your name, contact information, project location, budget, preferences, and the details of your interior design enquiry.

How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, discuss and plan projects, prepare estimates, provide our services, communicate with you, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Website services and external links

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests those resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. Links to WhatsApp, email, and telephone services take you to or open third-party services; their privacy practices are governed by those providers.

Cookies and analytics

We have not intentionally added advertising cookies or analytics to this website. Your browser or third-party services used to load content may still use their own technical storage or cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing and retention

We may share information with service providers when needed to communicate with you or deliver a requested service, or where disclosure is required by law. We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes and any applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and contact

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with us or request that we update or delete it, subject to legal and operational requirements. For privacy questions, email morwal.designs@gmail.com or call 0707 391 939.

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

This policy is a general website privacy notice. Morwal Interior Decors Solutions should review it against its actual data practices and applicable Kenyan privacy requirements before publication.
